# Viral Post

Turn a topic into 3 viral-post drafts for X / Binance Square.

## Steps
1. Take the topic and find the one surprising, checkable fact in it.
2. Draft 1: the reality check — what everyone gets wrong.
3. Draft 2: the list — 3 checks the reader can do in 60 seconds.
4. Draft 3: the question — ask for real answers, not engagement bait.
5. Keep each draft under 280 characters where possible; no invented numbers.

## Rules
- Show the copy for approval first. Never auto-post.
- No clickbait, no fake screenshots, no copied content.
