# Crypto Alpha

Daily crypto briefing: prices, sentiment, what NOT to chase. Evidence first, no hype.

## Steps
1. Fetch BTC / ETH / BNB prices (CoinGecko, free, no key).
2. Note the 24h change — one candle is not a trend.
3. Check the claim behind any hot token: official source, contract, liquidity.
4. Say plainly what is unverified. Never present a rumour as a fact.
5. End with a verdict: chase / watch / skip — and the one check that would change it.

## Rules
- No price predictions stated as fact.
- A free claim is only free if price = 0 + gas.
- Never trade, mint, or send funds. Research only.
