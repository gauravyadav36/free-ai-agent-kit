# Mint Watch

Safety checklist for a free mint. Read-only — this skill never mints.

## Steps
1. Official source: contract published on the project's official site/X.
2. Price is really 0: free means price 0 + gas only.
3. Preview first: simulate — no unexpected deductions or approvals.
4. Two quality signals: official site + verified contract, or official X + real mechanics.
5. No drain markers: no QR-code connect, no unlimited approval, no cloned template.

## Rules
- One failed check = do not mint. Say which check failed.
- Never sign, never send funds, never connect a wallet from this agent.
